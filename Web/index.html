About Deskew web

Deskew straightens scanned text documents: it finds the skew angle of the text lines and rotates the image so they are horizontal. This page runs the Deskew command line tool compiled to WebAssembly, so your images are processed on your computer and never uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Open an image or drop it anywhere on the view. The skew angle is detected right away.
  2. Check the result: Original shows the detection area (red, dashed) and the found content (green), Deskewed preview shows the rotated page and Work image the thresholded image Deskew actually looked at.
  3. Adjust options if needed and click Deskew & download to get the output file.

Tips

  • Zoom with the mouse wheel or a pinch, drag to pan, double click toggles between fit and 100 %.
  • Outcome other than detected means the angle is not reliable and the image is not rotated. Force rotation rotates it anyway.
  • Pages with dark scanner borders or photos often do better with Content area: Find automatically or with margins.
  • If the angle hits the limit (atRangeLimit), raise Max angle.
  • Faint or very dark scans may need an explicit Threshold; the work image shows what was considered to be text.
  • Extra params takes any other command line option, e.g. -f b1 for 1-bit output or -p 300 to override DPI. See the full list below.
  • Very large images may not fit into the memory available to WebAssembly (4 GB).
